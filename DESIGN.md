# DESIGN.md

Direction for the GitHub profile README. Authored by Habibi Ahmad Aziz, transcribed from his own words in the session of 2026-09-24. This file is direction, not instructions to an agent.

## Identity

A creative programmer. In the owner's words: *"seorang programmer biasanya wibu, dan wibu biasanya kreatif, berpikirlah seakan kamu programmer yang kreatif tapi tidak terlalu terlihat wibu."* Creative in the way fandom people are creative, without the profile announcing a fandom.

## Motif

Toothless and the Light Fury. They have survived every revision of this README because they are the one thing on the page that is actually his. They are the identity motif: a black dragon and a white one, paired so each reads against the other. They anchor the intro and close the page. They are not decoration to be swapped out.

## Personality

Creative, understated. The craft shows in the work, not in the volume of the page.

## Palette

Core: whatever GitHub's own theme gives (the page background and text color), so the profile belongs to the reader's theme rather than fighting it.

One accent: plasma violet `#A371F7`, the color of Toothless's plasma blast. One accent only, used at the moment that matters and nowhere else.

**Banned by the owner:** bright blue boxes, `capsule-render` banner bars, and anything that looks like them. His words: *"jangan gunakan kotak biru terang atau apapun yang serupa seperti ini, intronya jadi ga menarik banget."* Also banned earlier in the same session: `capsule-render` waving shapes, and fake code blocks presented as content.

## Typography and voice

English, direct, plain. Short sentences. No buzzwords, no "actually matters".

## Mood

`Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1`

- ENERGY 2: it says hello, it does not shout.
- RHYTHM 2: sections are consistent but deliberately not identical. Prose, table, icon row, and cards each get their own composition.
- MOTION 1: motion comes from the two dragons and one line of typed text. Nothing else moves.

## Open questions

The owner's site `habibiahmada.dev` is blocked by this session's network, so the About text, project list, and achievements are still the ones carried over from the previous README and have not been checked against the site.
